Community platform

One tap to reach support. A safer way to stay connected.

CommuNet is a community platform — an integrated ecosystem combining real-time safety support, trusted community, and personal resilience tools. It helps individuals and communities stay connected, supported, and resilient, in real life and online.

Free on Google Play and the App Store Android 7.0 or later iOS 15.1 or later

What it is

A platform for community preparedness, emergency coordination, and crisis response.

CommuNet is focused on improving community preparedness, emergency coordination, and crisis response. The aim is a practical platform that helps communities organize information, coordinate activities, manage readiness, and support decision-making before, during, and after incidents.

The problem it addresses

Many communities operate with a mixture of messaging groups, spreadsheets, phone calls, personal relationships, and institutional knowledge. Those tools may work under ordinary circumstances, but they often create challenges when information must be shared quickly, people must be coordinated, tasks must be assigned, readiness must be monitored, or situational awareness is required.

Those challenges become more acute during emergencies, when response speed, information accuracy, and coordination are critical.

CommuNet was also built in response to the growing rise in antisemitism and the need for safer, trusted spaces. In moments of uncertainty, you are not alone. With one tap, you can reach out and connect to help when you need it most.

FAQ

Common questions

What is CommuNet?

CommuNet is a community platform that combines real-time safety support, trusted community, and personal resilience tools. It is intended to help communities organize information, coordinate activities, manage readiness, and support decision-making before, during, and after incidents.

Who is it for?

The app offers verified spaces for students, organizations, travelers, and families. It is built for individuals and communities who want to stay connected, supported, and resilient.

How do I reach support quickly?

A built-in alert feature lets you quickly signal for help and connect to relevant support channels in critical moments. The official tagline is: “One tap to reach support.”

What can I report?

You can report incidents and contribute to a broader awareness system that helps strengthen community safety and visibility. Official screenshots show reporting categories for medical, fire, vandalism, and accident.

Is CommuNet free, and which devices does it support?

CommuNet is listed as free on both Google Play and the App Store. On Google Play it is a Communication app and requires Android 7.0 or later. On the App Store it is listed as Commu-net, in Social Networking, and requires iOS 15.1 or later. Version 2.1.23 is current on both stores. The Google Play listing was first published on April 9, 2026 and last updated on July 9, 2026. The App Store listing was first released on June 15, 2026, and the current iOS version is dated July 13, 2026. On Google Play the app is rated Everyone, with the notes “Users Interact, Shares Location.” On the App Store the content rating is 17+.

How is data handled?

On the Google Play Data safety section, the developer has declared that no data is collected and no data is shared with third parties. The Play listing also notes that the app shares location and lists location among its permissions. For the full policy, see the privacy policy.
